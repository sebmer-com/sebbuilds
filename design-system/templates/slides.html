One system, three canvases

Editorial content

Invariant

Preserve type roles, indexing, neutral contrast, rule hierarchy, and evidence labels. Adapt dimensions to the format instead of stretching one canvas into another.

  • Web: responsive publication rows and reading measures.
  • Slides: fixed masters with large evidence zones.
  • PDF: continuous reading rhythm and controlled page breaks.
Recognition comes from the grammar, not from copying dimensions.

The system is deliberately constrained

Evidence / Metrics
19 neutral color tokens across light and dark semantic roles
12 type roles spanning display, body, labels, code, slides, and print
0 gradients, glows, shadows, or decorative accent colors
Constraints make generated artifacts more recognizable and easier to review.
